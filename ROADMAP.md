# Roadmap

## Vision

Dottie Deeds exists to simplify complex California real estate document workflows through the thoughtful application of artificial intelligence.

The long-term objective is to reduce administrative work while preserving professional judgment. AI should automate repetitive processes, not replace expertise.

---

## Current Stage

**Private Beta**

Current development is focused on validating the platform with real users while improving reliability, usability, and document quality.

Current priorities include:

* Beta user onboarding
* Usage analytics
* User authentication
* Account management
* AI-assisted deed generation
* Production stability
* User feedback collection

---

## Long-Term Goals

* Multi-document transaction support
* Law firm administration tools
* Team collaboration
* Expanded California transaction workflows
* Additional jurisdictions
* AI-assisted transaction intelligence
* Workflow automation across the real estate transaction lifecycle

---

## Guiding Principles

Every product decision is evaluated against five core principles:

* Reduce operational friction.
* Preserve professional judgment.
* Prioritize security from day one.
* Build simple solutions to complex problems.
* Let real customer feedback guide product development.

---

## Looking Ahead

Dottie Deeds is still early in its journey.

The objective is not to build software with the greatest number of features.

The objective is to build software that legal professionals genuinely enjoy using because it saves time, reduces repetitive work, and fits naturally into existing workflows.

Every iteration is guided by real-world use, measurable feedback, and a commitment to solving meaningful problems for legal professionals.
