# Dottie Deeds

**Applied AI for California Real Estate Document Automation**

Dottie Deeds is an AI-powered legal workflow platform that helps attorneys and real estate professionals prepare California deed packages more efficiently.

The platform combines legal domain expertise, structured business logic, and large language models to automate repetitive document preparation while preserving professional review and judgment. Rather than replacing attorneys, Dottie is designed to eliminate administrative work so legal professionals can spend more time solving client problems.

---

# Why I Built Dottie

After more than fifteen years practicing business and real estate law, I realized that preparing deeds and related transfer documents remained one of the most repetitive workflows in an otherwise sophisticated legal practice.

The legal analysis often required significant expertise.

The document preparation did not.

Modern AI created an opportunity to rethink that workflow from the ground up.

Dottie is the product I always wished existed while practicing law.

---

# The Problem

Preparing California real estate transfer documents is still surprisingly manual.

Attorneys and paralegals routinely:

* Gather client and property information
* Determine the appropriate transfer mechanism
* Draft multiple recording instruments
* Verify statutory language
* Assemble county recording packages
* Produce Word and PDF deliverables
* Proofread formatting and execution requirements

While each transaction is unique, much of the document preparation follows repeatable patterns that can be significantly streamlined through software.

---

# The Solution

Dottie combines structured legal workflows with AI-assisted document generation to reduce repetitive work while maintaining attorney oversight.

Current areas of focus include:

* Grant Deeds
* Quitclaim Deeds
* Interspousal Transfer Deeds
* Trust Transfers
* LLC and Entity Transfers
* Recording package preparation
* Intelligent document assembly

The objective is not to automate legal judgment.

The objective is to automate everything surrounding it.

# Product Principles

Every design decision is guided by five principles:

* AI should augment professionals, not replace them.
* Legal workflows require transparency and explainability.
* Security must be designed into the product from day one.
* Simplicity beats feature bloat.
