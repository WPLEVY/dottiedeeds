Dottie is a software provider. Dottie is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not enter into an attorney-client relationship with any user or any user's client. 3. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information, depending on how you interact with the Service:

  • Account information: your name, email address, firm name, and login credentials.
  • Document information: the information you enter to generate documents, such as party names, property addresses, legal descriptions, and related details.
  • Uploaded files: documents you upload, such as a prior recorded deed, from which the Service extracts information.
  • Usage information: basic analytics about how the Service is used, such as pages viewed and documents generated.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: used to keep you signed in and to measure usage.
  • Communications: information you provide when you contact us for support, submit a form, respond to a survey, or otherwise correspond with us, including the contents of your messages.
  • Billing and payment information: subscription plan, billing contact details, and transaction records. Payment card details are collected and processed directly by our third-party payment processor; we do not store full payment card numbers on our systems.
  • Device and log information: technical information automatically collected when you use the Service, such as IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, referring pages, and timestamps.
  • Client and third-party information: information relating to your clients or other individuals (such as grantors, grantees, and other parties to a deed) that you choose to submit. You are responsible for having a lawful basis and any necessary rights or consents to provide such information to us.
