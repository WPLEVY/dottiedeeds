Last updated: June 2026 · Dottie Deeds
Our Commitment

Dottie Deeds is built by attorneys, for attorneys. We understand the sensitivity of legal work and client confidentiality. We collect only what we need to operate the service, we do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties except as described in this policy.

Section 1Who We Are

Dottie Deeds ("we," "us," "our") is a California legal technology company providing document drafting software for California-licensed attorneys. Our service is located at dottiedeeds.com. For privacy questions, contact us at info@dottiedeeds.com.

Section 3Document and Data Retention Design

Dottie Deeds is designed to avoid permanent retention of uploaded documents and generated document content except as necessary to operate, secure, maintain, troubleshoot, improve, or comply with legal obligations.

Specifically, we design our systems so that:

  • Uploaded prior deeds are processed through our AI extraction service and are not intentionally retained beyond the extraction session
  • Generated document content is returned to your browser for download and is not intentionally stored on our servers as a permanent record
  • Client-identifying information entered into document fields is not collected or stored by us

Notwithstanding the above, standard infrastructure operations — including server logs, backups, caching, security monitoring, and cloud vendor practices — may result in transient copies of data being retained for limited periods. Such copies are not used for any purpose other than operating, securing, and maintaining the service.

Section 4How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Dottie Deeds service
  • Process your subscription and send billing communications
  • Send service-related communications including trial expiration notices and feature updates
  • Respond to your support requests and questions
  • Detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Analyze aggregate usage patterns to improve the service

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not share your information with other law firms or legal service providers.

Section 6Attorney-Client Confidentiality

We understand that attorneys have strict confidentiality obligations to their clients under the California Rules of Professional Conduct. Dottie Deeds is designed with this in mind:

  • Document generation occurs in your browser — client matter details are not intentionally transmitted to or permanently stored on our servers
  • We recommend that attorneys review their firm's data security policies before using any cloud-based software in connection with client matter information
  • Attorneys are solely responsible for ensuring that their use of this Service complies with their professional obligations regarding client confidentiality

Section 7Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period thereafter for legal and business purposes. Specifically:

  • Account data — retained for the duration of your subscription plus 3 years
  • Billing records — retained for 7 years as required by law
  • Uploaded PDFs — not intentionally retained beyond the AI extraction session
  • Generated documents — not intentionally stored on our servers
  • Usage logs — retained for 12 months then anonymized

Section 8No Guarantee of Complete Deletion

While we honor deletion requests where legally and technically feasible, certain information may remain in backup systems, security logs, legal archives, billing systems, or disaster recovery systems for a limited period following a deletion request. Such residual copies are subject to the protections of this policy and will not be used for any active purpose.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting info@dottiedeeds.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days to the extent legally and technically feasible.

Section 9Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted to and from the service
  • Secure, encrypted storage for account and billing information
  • Access controls limiting who within our organization can access user data
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure and third-party providers

No security system is impenetrable. Users acknowledge that electronic transmission and storage involve inherent risks. Dottie Deeds cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of information will never occur. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

Section 10Business Transfers

If Dottie Deeds undergoes a merger, acquisition, financing transaction, asset sale, reorganization, or similar corporate event, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law. We will provide notice of any such transfer that results in a material change to this Privacy Policy.

Section 11Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access — You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — You may request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion — You may request deletion of your account and personal data, subject to Section 8
  • Portability — You may request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out — You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time

Section 12California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • The right to delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions
  • The right to opt out of the sale of your personal information — we do not sell personal information
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

Section 13International Users

Dottie Deeds is operated from the United States and is intended primarily for users located in the United States. Users accessing the Service from other jurisdictions do so at their own initiative and are responsible for compliance with applicable local laws. By using the Service, international users consent to the processing and storage of their information in the United States, which may have different data protection standards than their home jurisdiction.

Section 14Cookies

Dottie Deeds uses minimal cookies and local browser storage necessary to operate the service — specifically to remember your firm settings and session state between visits. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies.

Section 15Children's Privacy

Dottie Deeds is intended for use by licensed legal professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email at least 14 days before they take effect. The date at the top of this policy reflects when it was last updated. Continued use of the service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
