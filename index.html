Why Dottie

Everything your paralegal needs.
Nothing they don't.

AI autofill from prior deeds

Upload the prior recorded deed as a PDF. Dottie reads it and fills grantor, APN, legal description, and county, so your paralegal starts from a draft, not a blank page.

Correct notary blocks

Individual, trustee, attorney-in-fact, corporate officer, sheriff. Dottie places the right California acknowledgment or jurat, with the venue aligned, every time.

PCOR generation

A pre-filled BOE-502-A alongside every applicable deed, drawn from all 58 county assessors, so the recorder's added fee for a missing PCOR never applies.

58-county paralegal guide

Recording fees, recorder contacts, e-recording vendors, and the common rejection reasons for every California county, built into the workflow.

Firm settings & custom provisions

Set your firm details once and write your own reusable provisions. Every document pre-populates with your firm's information, and your settings follow you across devices.

Saved documents

Save any matter and reopen it later to edit or regenerate. Your work is stored per firm, isolated so only your firm can see it, and always ready to export to Word or text.
